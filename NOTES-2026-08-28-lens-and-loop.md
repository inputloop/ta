### READING (mine — offered for ratification, not canon)

1. **The page is one loop with two rails.** PROMPT enters at the top and LEARN is the
   axis running down between them. The **Mechanical** rail is what can be counted
   (tests, quality-to-enter, tokens, time); the **Logic** rail is what must be judged
   (success criteria to exit, doubt, a guess at performance expressed as *% non-functional*).
   Counting alone cannot exit the loop; judgment alone cannot enter it.
2. **The four-beat cycle is GUESS → TRY → TEST → WRITE**, and the value is not in any
   beat — it is in the **DELTA between the guess and the actual.** That is why GUESS is
   beat 1 and not an afterthought: a try with no prior guess produces a result but no
   information about the guesser. *This is the load-bearing idea on the page.*
3. **"% NON-FUNCT" is the honest denominator.** Guessing performance as a percentage
   *non-functional* (rather than percent working) keeps the estimate pointed at the
   failure, which is the number that can be checked.
4. **"AVOID HAND EDITS / MARKDOWN"** is a reproducibility rule, not a style
   preference: if the artifact is hand-edited between beats, the delta measured at
   TEST is no longer the delta the guess was about. The pipeline must regenerate.
5. **The bottom band is the delivery tier.** TOOL and DATA are separate; local data
   flows to OUTPUT, and only OUTPUT is shared. The `MD` box — *model files, static
   argument, adds to vendor model* — is the operating-model kit itself, named in its
   mechanical role: a static argument prepended to whatever vendor model is in play.
6. **"MORE, ACCURATE, FASTER EACH TIME"** is the loop's success criterion in three
   measures, and it is exactly the shape that goes volumetric if unguarded — "more"
   will happily be satisfied by volume alone. See `MEASURES.md`.
7. **"Tell me: each time / when you …"** is the telemetry contract with the operator —
   the agent announces at named beats rather than being asked.

- **Q-2 ANSWERED by Corwin 2026-08-29: *access*.** Not *assess*. So the line is a
  **constraint, not an act**: on the mechanical rail, only the measures you can actually
  reach count. That is the stronger of the two readings — it makes **reachability part of
  the measure** rather than a precondition of taking one, and it sits correctly under
  Tokens and Time, which are exactly the measures that are always reachable and therefore
  always tempting. Consequence for `MEASURES.md`: a measure nobody can reach is not a
  conservative measure, it is an absent one, and `UNMEASURED` is the honest word for it.

```
                  /     |         \
                 /     FEEL        \
                /       v           \

              U N D E R S T A N D I N G

CHANGE & LEARNING are two "opposable" contextually definable
(generally measurable!) properties of any (otherwise) suitable
context to measures

Create "opposition" (as measurement constructs) BY LEARNING
ACCORDING TO DELTA-√  ( c((c s/p)) / (∂p/∂F) ? )  by viewing
bearing (materials) recipted as useful at points of
success/fail ~ change per vibe.

Where: vibe is feeling as actually expressable in given some context
```

The formula fragment is transcribed as drawn and is **not** interpreted below; it
carries his own `?`. The word before "as useful" reads *recipted* — his coinage from
*receipt*, and I have kept it.

### READING (mine — offered for ratification)

1. **The figure is an hourglass, and the waist is the point.** FOCUS is the upper
   bowl, UNDERSTANDING the lower. **CHANGE** enters the waist from the left carrying
   "SYSTEM"; **LEARN** enters from the right carrying "PERSON". Everything narrows to
   the waist and opens again — you cannot get focus or understanding except by passing
   through the single place where a system change and a person's learning meet.
2. **The four points are the two axes crossed.** Upward from the waist: **RESULT** and
   **SHOW** (with PLAN·CONFIDENCE) produce FOCUS. Downward: **PLAN** and **CLARITY**
   (with FEEL) produce UNDERSTANDING. So *focus is what you can show*, and
   *understanding is what you can feel your way through a plan with.* The dialectic is
   not thesis/antithesis — it is **system-change against person-learning**, and the
   extrapolation is what you get by pushing on both.
3. **"Opposable" is a design instruction, not an observation.** CHANGE and LEARNING are
   *made* opposable so they can be measured against each other; the opposition is a
   **measurement construct** you create, not a fact you find. That is the sentence I
   would build the tooling from.
4. **Start from a risky fact.** Item 1 says the fact or assumption should *ideally* be
   risky or controversial — because a safe assumption produces no delta when tested, and
   the whole apparatus is delta-powered. This is the same instinct as the kit's
   "walk toward the weak point."
5. **"Recipted at points of success/fail"** — the receipt attaches where the outcome
   turns, not uniformly. Cheap, and it puts the evidence exactly where an argument will
   later be had.
6. **VIBE is given a working definition** — *feeling as actually expressable in some
   given context* — which makes it admissible as data rather than mood: not "how it
   feels" but "how much of the feeling this context lets you state." That is a
   measurable ceiling, and an unusual one.
